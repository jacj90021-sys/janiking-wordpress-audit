## Notes

- The 120 US location pages and ~100 franchise location pages are data-driven
  (same template, different content per city).
- International locations are listed on `/locations/` as external regional
  domains (janiking.ca, janiking.co.uk, janiking.pt, etc.) — not recreated as
  local pages.
- Campaign pages (`/peak/`, `/pristine/`, `/spring-clean-challenge/`, etc.) are
  low-priority legacy/marketing pages.
- `/make-an-appointment/`, `/privacy-policy-3/` are legacy pages.
